Why mechanisation is moving indoors

A machine that grows food, not just a garden.

Mechanisation usually means tractors built for volume crops in open fields. High-value produce needs consistent, controlled output, so we build the tower farm as agricultural machinery: a frame, a power supply, a control system and automated delivery.

  • Consistent, controlled output
  • Built for HORECA & retail buyers
  • Engineered like machinery
  • Scales tower by tower

The machine

How our system works

Four subsystems, working together as one growing machine.

  1. 01 · Structure

    Aeroponic towers

    Vertical towers that grow upward, not outward.

  2. 02 · Power

    Solar

    Runs pumps and sensors, independent of the grid.

  3. 03 · Control

    IoT sensing & monitoring

    Sensors track conditions, monitored remotely.

  4. 04 · Delivery

    Automated dosing

    Nutrients and water misted onto roots on schedule.

Why aeroponics? The machinery logic

Water efficiency

Roots are misted, not flooded, and water is recirculated.

Automation fit

Every input is measurable, so it can be automated.

Density

More plants per square metre, grown close to buyers.

About Us

Agritech from Lagos, building machines that grow food.

Growing machinery
for Nigerian agribusiness

Gaia Labs is a Lagos-based agritech company building automated growing machinery for Nigerian agribusiness. Our solar-powered, IoT-monitored aeroponic towers grow premium leafy greens for HORECA, retail and institutional buyers, with consistent output all year round.

Developed in Lagos,
built to scale

We're bringing equipment, irrigation and fertigation, automation and energy together into one operation. Next, we're building a local supply chain for components, solar, nutrients and maintenance, so every tower can be assembled, run and repaired close to where it grows.
